## Reading the list

The cheapest fruit in the shop is Rocket Fruit at $5,000 Beli, followed by Spin Fruit at $7,500 and Blade Fruit at $30,000. The most expensive fruit on this list is Smoke Fruit at $100,000. Even at the top of this list you are paying under one percent of the cost of Dragon Fruit.

## Practical advice

- Buy one cheap fruit to learn how fruit moves feel, then save for the next step.
- Check the Robux column before you spend real money. Small fruits are cheap in Robux too, from R$50 up to R$250 in this group.
- Remember a common fruit is not a trade value. Players trade on demand, and the shop price is only the starting number.

For the mid-range, see the [rare and uncommon fruit prices](https://blox-fruits.resuhive.com/rarity/rare) and the [common fruit page](https://blox-fruits.resuhive.com/rarity/common).
