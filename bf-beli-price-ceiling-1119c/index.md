# Dragon Fruit Beli Price and the Top of the Blox Fruits Shop

The Blox Fruits shop has a clear ceiling. Values were last synced on November 15, 2026, and the catalogue lists 42 fruits for sale. At the very top sits Dragon Fruit at 15,000,000 Beli, which is three times the price of the next fruit down. At the bottom is Rocket Fruit at 5,000 Beli, a thousand times cheaper. Understanding where a fruit sits on that ladder is useful before you spend millions of Beli on a single purchase, because a high shop tag does not automatically mean a good trade.

## The three most expensive fruits

The [full Blox Fruits beli price list](https://blox-fruits.resuhive.com/fruit-prices) runs from 5,000 Beli to 15,000,000 Beli. Dragon Fruit is first at R$5,000 in Robux. Control Fruit is second at 9,000,000 Beli and Kitsune Fruit third at 8,000,000 Beli. Both cost R$4,000. Those three are the only fruits in the S tier of the [trade value tier list](https://blox-fruits.resuhive.com/tier-list), which begins at 8,000,000. The page for [Dragon Fruit beli price](https://blox-fruits.resuhive.com/values/dragon-fruit) shows it is ranked number one of 74 valued items and was added in update 13. Its page also notes that it is worth about 1.7 times a Control Fruit, which matches the gap between their two shop tags.

## The middle of the ladder

Below the top three, prices fall quickly. Magnet Fruit is 6,000,000 Beli, Tiger and Yeti are 5,000,000 each, Spirit is 3,400,000 and Gas is 3,200,000. The [Control Fruit beli price](https://blox-fruits.resuhive.com/values/control-fruit) page confirms the 9,000,000 tag and the R$4,000 Robux price. A player working through the A tier, which covers 3,200,000 to 8,000,000, will recognise most of those names. The B tier is much wider with 27 items between 1,000,000 and 3,200,000, and includes fighting styles such as Godhuman and Sanguine Art as well as fruits.

## Shop price and trade value are different things

This is the part that catches new players. The [Kitsune Fruit beli price](https://blox-fruits.resuhive.com/values/kitsune-fruit) is 8,000,000, but the game has no official trade value, so what another player will give you can drift away from that number. The site keeps a separate value column for 74 items and marks each one stable or moving, with the sync date shown at the top of every page. Thirteen items have no value and are left out of the tier list. Treat the Beli tag as a baseline and check the value page before a large trade.

## A practical way to use the list

Pick a budget first, then work down from the most expensive fruit you can afford. If you have 10,000,000 Beli you can reach Dragon, but Control at 9,000,000 leaves room for a second purchase, and Kitsune at 8,000,000 leaves even more. Read the shop tag, open the value page, and only then decide. The list is rebuilt from the Blox Fruits Wiki and the numbers are labelled as shop prices rather than trade prices, so there is no hidden claim that a fruit is worth more than the game says.
