# A Field Guide to Books in Order

People looking up <author> books in order and series reading orders usually want the same thing: a page that gives the answer without making them dig. A working checklist, organised by the question you are actually trying to answer rather than by the site's own menu. The pages below are all on Books in Order and were picked because each one answers a different part of that question. None of them require an account, and none of them bury the useful part below a wall of text. If you only read one section, read the second one - that is the page most visitors are actually looking for. The rest are there for when the simple answer is not enough and you want to check the detail yourself.

## Before you go

These four pages are a starting map, not the whole site. Books in Order keeps adding and refreshing material, so the page that was newest when this was written will not be the newest one for long. The reliable approach is to pick the page that matches your question, check the date or the update note on it, and only then use the answer. Bookmark the one you come back to most often and ignore the rest - most readers need two or three pages, not all of them. If a link looks out of date, the [Books in Order homepage](https://books.resuhive.com/) is the quickest way back into the current material.
