# Two Recent Furniture and Home Goods Recalls Explained: Remedies and What to Check

If you own anything in the furniture and home goods category, here are two recent U.S. recalls to compare against what is in your house, with the model details and the remedy the company is offering. For the full list, see the latest [CPSC product recalls](https://recalls.resuhive.com/) on Recall Radar, which summarizes each official notice.

## 1. HAUOMS Eight-drawer Dressers recall

Recall number 27068, announced November 5, 2026. Product: HAUOMS Eight-drawer Dressers. Units affected: About 1,392.

**The hazard.** The recalled dressers are unstable if they are not anchored to the wall, posing tip-over and entrapment hazards that can result in risks of serious injuries or death to children. The dressers violate the mandatory safety standards as required by the STURDY Act. None reported

**Which products.** This recall involves HAUOM-branded Eight-drawer dressers. The wooden black dressers measure about 27.6 inches wide, 15.8 inches long, 59.1 inches tall, and weigh about 112.4 pounds. "SKU: AJ_DJ504298_1" is printed on the product packaging.

**What to do.** Consumers should stop using the recalled dressers immediately if they are not anchored to the wall, place them in an area that children cannot access and contact Jiastfeng for a full refund. Consumers will be asked to write in permanent marker “RECALLED” on the top, front and sides of the dresser, remove all drawers and send photos of the marked and disassembled dresser to the contact email listed in the notice . Consumers should then dispose of the product. Contact: Jiastfeng by email at the contact email listed in the notice .

The full summary is on the [HAUOMS Eight-drawer Dressers recall page](https://recalls.resuhive.com/recall/hauoms-eight-drawer-dressers-27068/), with a check-if-you-own-it section and links to the official notice.

## 2. Little Giant ladders with Rapid Locks recall

Recall number 27067, announced November 5, 2026. Product: Little Giant ladders with Rapid Locks. Units affected: About 3,662,000.

**The hazard.** The ladders can suddenly retract if the Rapid Locks are not fully engaged, posing a fall hazard to consumers. Little Giant has received 25 reports of the ladders retracting, including 19 reports of injuries, consisting of three reports of head injuries such as traumatic brain injury, skull fracture, concussion, vision loss, hearing loss and cognitive impairment; and 16 reports of other injuries such as joint or bone fracture, lacerations and bruising. Little Giant has also received 39 reports of Rapid Locks not working properly.

**Which products.** This recall involves Little Giant ladders with Rapid Locks. Rapid Locks are designed to insert into ladder rungs for the purpose of adjusting the ladder’s height. Rapid Locks are black or orange and are located on each ladder leg. The affected models are Adjustable Fortress 2.0, Conquest 2.0, Dark Horse 2.0, Epic, King Kombo XT, Leveler, MegaLite, MegaLite+, Multi, Quantum and Vector. The brand name Little Giant and the model name are located on the side of the ladder. Only ladders without an orange rectangular “QC” sticker on the rail above one of the Rapid Locks are included in this recall.

**What to do.** Consumers should stop using the recalled ladders immediately until they review the information at www.littlegiantladders.com/pages/recalls or via the QR code on the ladder and contact Little Giant for a repair kit containing warning labels and instructions. The additional instructions and labels, which should be affixed to the ladder, advise consumers to verify that their Rapid Locks are properly engaged before every use and to maintain their locks with lubrication. Consumers who follow the provided instructions and have a Rapid Lock that does not function properly after the application of lubrication should contact Little Giant for evaluation. If a lock does not function properly after this evaluation, the company will offer the choice of a replacement ladder of the same or equivalent model or a refund ranging from a depreciated value determined by date of purchase up to full purchase price for ladders purchased within the past 12 months. Contact: Little Giant toll-free at 866-201-9069 from 6 a.m. to 3 p.m. MT Monday through Friday or visit www.littlegiantladders.com/pages/recalls or www.littlegiantladders.com and click on RECALLS at the top of the home page.

The full summary is on the [Little Giant ladders with Rapid Locks recall page](https://recalls.resuhive.com/recall/ladders-with-rapid-locks-27067/), with a check-if-you-own-it section and links to the official notice.

## A quick routine for any recall

Find the model number or date code on the label, compare it with the notice, stop using the product if it matches, and use the free remedy. You can browse every [furniture and home goods recall](https://recalls.resuhive.com/category/baby-kids/) or filter by hazard type on Recall Radar. Official notices from the U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission are always the authority; these pages summarize them to save you reading time, and the same CPSC product recalls feed is updated as new notices come in.

Recalls are free to act on, and acting quickly matters most for products used by children or left running overnight, such as heaters, chargers and sleepwear.
